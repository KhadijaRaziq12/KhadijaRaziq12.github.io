I am a molecular geneticist and postdoctoral researcher at Anhui Agricultural University, Hefei, China, with a PhD in Genetics from Harbin Medical University. My research spans cancer genetics, non-coding RNAs (miRNA and lncRNA), nanovaccination, antimicrobial biomaterials, and the molecular mechanisms of chemotherapy resistance in gastric and gastrointestinal cancers.

I bring strong theoretical and practical expertise in human genetics, molecular biology, in vivo cancer models, and advanced imaging techniques including flow cytometry and confocal microscopy. As a highly motivated researcher and educator, I am passionate about developing innovative therapeutic strategies — including immuno- and chemo-therapies, nanovaccines, and metal complex-based antimicrobials — for cancer and infectious diseases.

Research Areas

Cancer Genetics

Molecular mechanisms underlying cancer development, progression, and chemoresistance, with focus on gastric and gastrointestinal cancers.

Non-coding RNA

Role of miRNA and lncRNA in gene regulation, cancer biology, and competitive endogenous RNA (ceRNA) networks.

Nanovaccination

Advancing nanovaccination strategies for combinatory immune regulation therapies to treat infectious diseases and cancer.

Antimicrobial Biomaterials

Design and evaluation of novel nanocomposites, metal complexes, and photodynamic therapies for biofilm disruption and anti-infective treatment.

In Vivo Cancer Models

Mice handling, tumor induction, and preclinical evaluation of therapeutic strategies using animal models.

Nanomaterial Characterization

Characterization of nanomaterials and biomaterials using advanced imaging and spectroscopy techniques including confocal microscopy and flow cytometry.
